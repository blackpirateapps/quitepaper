3. Zero-Knowledge Encryption & User Key Custody

Quiet Paper operates on a strict zero-knowledge architecture using client-side cryptographic primitives (Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD). By using Cloud Sync, you acknowledge and agree to the following technical and operational realities:

  • Sole Custody of Encryption Credentials: You are the sole custodian of your Quiet Paper encryption password and emergency recovery key. Neither your encryption password nor your plaintext master key is ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Unrecoverable Loss: If you forget your encryption password and lose your emergency recovery key, your encrypted data is permanently cryptographically unrecoverable by anyone, including the developers and maintainers of Quiet Paper. We cannot reset your encryption password, decrypt your notes, or issue replacement master keys.
  • Separate Authentication & Encryption Passwords: Resetting your Firebase account password restores account authentication access, but will not decrypt your notes if your encryption password or recovery key is lost.

Because the app is offline-first, any interruption to cloud synchronization does not prevent you from accessing, writing, searching, or exporting notes stored locally on your device.
