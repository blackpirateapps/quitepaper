SECTION 01

Our Core Philosophy

Most modern productivity and note-taking services treat your private thoughts, journals, and drafts as proprietary training data or ad-targeting signals. They store plaintext databases on centralized servers and ask you to trust their legal terms not to misuse your private documents.

Zero-Knowledge Security Invariant

Quiet Paper does not ask you to trust our intent. We rely on cryptographic math. All note titles, bodies, tags, and attachments are encrypted on your physical device before entering transit. Our servers are crypto-blind: we do not possess the keys to decrypt your notes, and we cannot read them even if compelled by third parties.

Quiet Paper is designed from the ground up as an offline-first application. Cloud synchronization is entirely optional. If you never configure an account or cloud sync, not a single byte of your note data ever leaves your hardware.

SECTION 02

Offline-First & Local Persistence

When you create, edit, organize, or search notes in Quiet Paper, all operations execute locally on your device:

  • Local SQLite Database: Notes, metadata, tags, and folder hierarchies are stored in an app-private SQLite database managed via Drift.
  • On-Device Search & Indexing: Full-text search and OCR live-text extraction run 100% locally. Search queries are never sent to remote servers.
  • Manual & Rolling Backups (.qpbackup): If you create notebook backups or enable automated daily rolling snapshots, the archive file is saved directly to your device storage using Android's native Storage Access Framework (SAF). You choose whether backups are encrypted with your password via Argon2id + XChaCha20-Poly1305.
  • Encrypted Local Storage: Cryptographic master keys, authentication tokens, and backup passwords cached on-device are protected using platform hardware security modules (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain via FlutterSecureStorage).
SECTION 03

Zero-Knowledge Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to enable Quiet Paper Cloud Sync to synchronize notes between multiple devices, your data is protected by a multi-layered cryptographic envelope:

How Your Notes Are Encrypted

  1. Random Master Key: A cryptographically secure 256-bit secret key is generated directly on your device. This key never leaves your device in plaintext.
  2. Argon2id Key Derivation: Your Quiet Paper encryption password is processed using Argon2id (memory: 19MB, iterations: 2, salt: 16 random bytes) to derive a 256-bit wrapping key.
  3. Wrapped Master Key: The master key is wrapped with the password key using XChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption and uploaded to the server vault.
  4. Payload Encryption: Every note title, body, and tag list is serialized into JSON and encrypted using your Master Key and XChaCha20-Poly1305 with a fresh 24-byte random nonce and authenticated associated data (AAD) bound to the note ID.
Data Category Transmitted to Cloud Storage State on Server Security Guarantee
Note Title & Body Encrypted Ciphertext blob (content_ciphertext) Zero-knowledge; server cannot read
Tags & Categories Encrypted Ciphertext blob (inside encrypted payload) Zero-knowledge; server cannot inspect tags
Master Encryption Key Encrypted Ciphertext (wrapped_master_key) Wrapped client-side with Argon2id; inaccessible to server
Note ID & Timestamps Plaintext UUID Metadata columns (id, updated_at) Required for cursor delta sync and conflict resolution
Account Email Plaintext Firebase Auth identity record Required for identity authentication & password reset
Search Queries Never Not transmitted 100% offline on-device execution

Separation of Passwords: Your account sign-in password (managed by Firebase) authenticates your identity ("who you are"). Your Quiet Paper encryption password derives the key that unlocks your vault ("can you read notes"). The server never receives or stores your encryption password.

SECTION 04

Information We Process & Legal Basis

We process the absolute minimum data required to deliver the core note-taking and synchronization features:

A. Account Information (Cloud Sync Users Only)

When you register for Cloud Sync, we collect your email address and generate an opaque user identification token. This information is processed under the legal basis of contractual necessity (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) to authenticate your account across your devices and prevent unauthorized access.

B. Encrypted Synchronization Records

When syncing, our serverless backend receives and stores:

  • Encrypted ciphertext payloads and nonces.
  • Opaque UUID identifiers for notes.
  • Timestamps (createdAt, updatedAt, deletedAt) and monotonic revision counters used solely to determine sync order and resolve conflicting edits across devices.
  • Lifecycle flags (archived, trashed, pinned) to optimize multi-device state syncing.

C. Crash Reports & Diagnostics

Quiet Paper does not embed automatic third-party crash reporting SDKs. If you report an issue via GitHub or email, any logs or diagnostics you provide are shared voluntarily and used exclusively to diagnose and resolve software bugs.

SECTION 05

What We Never Collect or Sell

We believe digital privacy requires explicit technical boundaries. Specifically, Quiet Paper:

  • Never sells, rents, or monetizes user data: We have never sold user information and will never sell personal information under any circumstance.
  • Zero advertising SDKs or tracking beacons: There are no advertising frameworks (Google AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, etc.) or behavioural marketing trackers in Quiet Paper.
  • Zero analytics trackers: No third-party behavioral analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, Flurry, Amplitude) are bundled in the application.
  • Zero advertising identifiers: The app does not collect the Google Advertising ID (AAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • Zero unencrypted content access: We cannot view, inspect, or process the text, titles, or images contained within your encrypted notes.
SECTION 06

Third-Party Infrastructure & Subprocessors

To provide high-availability synchronization and secure cloud infrastructure, we partner with reputable cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Used exclusively to manage user identities, provide secure session tokens, and enable account password resets. Google acts as a data processor subject to standard contractual clauses.
  • Turso / libSQL (ChiselStrike Inc.): Distributed cloud SQLite database where encrypted ciphertext blobs and sync revision change logs are stored. Turso operates as a crypto-blind storage tier.
  • Vercel Inc.: Hosts the serverless API router (handling authenticated REST endpoints) and the static landing page. Vercel processes transient HTTPS requests in transit without persisting user data.
  • GitHub (Microsoft Corp.): Hosts the open-source source code repository and public application APK release binaries.
SECTION 07

Your Rights Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, & Global Regulations

Regardless of your geographic location, Quiet Paper extends universal digital privacy rights to all users:

Right to Data Portability & Export

Your content is never locked in a proprietary database format. You can export all your notes at any time as open-standard Markdown (.md) files with standard YAML frontmatter, directly to any folder on your device. You can also generate complete .qpbackup snapshot archives.

Right to Erasure & Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your data at any time:

  • Local Erasure: Deleting notes in the app or clearing application storage permanently deletes all local records from your SQLite database.
  • Remote Cloud Deletion: Permanently emptying your Trash sends cryptographic deletion tombstones that purge associated ciphertext records from the sync database.
  • Full Account Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your sync account by emailing contact@blackpiratex.com. Upon verification, your Firebase user record and all associated encrypted cloud database records are permanently purged within 30 days.

Emergency Recovery Keys & Cryptographic Responsibility

During sync configuration, Quiet Paper generates an emergency recovery key. Please store this key securely. Because Quiet Paper operates on a zero-knowledge model, if you forget your Quiet Paper encryption password and lose your emergency recovery key, our staff has zero technical capability to recover or reset your encrypted vault. This is an unavoidable consequence of genuine encryption.
